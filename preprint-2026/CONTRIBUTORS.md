# Contributions and scientific scope

Nicolás Sáez developed the computational and mathematical analysis, simulation code, uncertainty analyses, visualizations and manuscript for this preprint.

The field problem was initially identified by Germán Sepúlveda (project direction) and by Germán Sepúlveda and Mabel Arismendi (field visits and photographic documentation). Both approved acknowledgement and single-author presentation of the revised computational preprint, according to their response supplied to the corresponding author.

Their photographs and the original field records are not part of this software deposit. The work is a modeling study; it does not constitute a taxonomically confirmed observation of *P. menthae* in Chile.
