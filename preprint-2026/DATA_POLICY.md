# Data and copyright policy

The deposited `results/` files are stochastic model outputs and not original DMC/INIA station datasets. Meteorological series are not included. Obtain source observations from the Dirección Meteorológica de Chile / INIA (RE1018) subject to applicable terms, and cite the stations and the data provider. Scripts automate download and cleaning where accessible. Do not upload field photographs or unpublished project materials without their owners' consent.
