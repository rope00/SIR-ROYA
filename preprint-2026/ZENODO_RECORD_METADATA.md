Description:
This research-software archive contains Python code and model-derived simulation outputs
for a spatial stochastic susceptible--exposed--infectious model assessing potential
Puccinia menthae epidemic scenarios for oregano-growing areas of northern Chile.
It includes a 50-by-50 lattice simulation implementation, weather download and
quality-control scripts, a structured 36-setting parameter sweep, a reconstructed
historical NetLogo-rule diagnostic, and a seven-setting Monte Carlo convergence
study comprising 10,500 runs. The study is exploratory: pathogen occurrence in the
Chilean localities was not independently confirmed, and the model is not calibrated
to disease surveys. Meteorological observations from DMC/INIA, external photographs
and private documents are excluded from the deposit. Data source details and
reproduction instructions are described in the README.
